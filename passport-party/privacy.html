Passport Party

Privacy Policy

Passport Party is a trivia and timing game with on-device guest saves, live room sync, and optional in-app purchases. This page explains what the current build stores, what it sends to service providers, and how players can remove their local saved data.

No account required No analytics SDK Live room sync via Supabase Purchases via RevenueCat

What this app stores on your device

  • The nickname a player types into the app.
  • Guest progress such as score, passport stamps, seen stops, selected language, and last saved run state.
  • Local room details such as the most recent room code shown on the device.

What this app sends to the live multiplayer backend

  • Your nickname and generated player ID when you create or join a live room.
  • The room code and lobby you joined.
  • Room status such as waiting, countdown, live, and finished.
  • Submitted answers, timing taps, stop results, and score updates.
  • Shared session data such as route order and room history.

Optional purchases

  • Passport Party uses RevenueCat to load in-app purchase products, validate purchases, restore purchases, and keep anonymous purchase status.
  • RevenueCat may process purchase history and an anonymous app user ID for app functionality and purchase validation.
  • Apple handles App Store payment details. Passport Party does not receive payment card numbers.

What this build does not do

  • It does not require sign-in or account creation.
  • It does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking in the current build.
  • It does not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data.

Third-party services used in this build

Supabase is used for live lobby sync, room state, and multiplayer match data.

RevenueCat is used for in-app purchase product loading, purchase validation, restore purchase support, and anonymous purchase status.

Sharing

If a player taps the share button for a private room, the app opens the system share sheet so the player can send the room code through another app they choose. Passport Party does not receive or store that message.

Data retention and deletion

  • Guest progress stays on the device until it is removed.
  • Using Reset Guest Save inside the app deletes the stored nickname and local progress on the device.
  • Live room and match rows already written to the multiplayer backend are not removed by Reset Guest Save.
  • To request deletion of live backend rows associated with your nickname or generated player ID, contact support@duvdex.com.
  • Deleting the app also removes any remaining local app data from the device.

Children

Passport Party is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal data from children.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact support@duvdex.com.
